About

A builder who
became a leader.

I started by hand-coding training content for Apple's shop floor. Two decades later I lead content services for QuickBooks, with AI acceleration on top. The through-line never changed: make knowledge work harder, for more people.

I've always sat at the seam between content, engineering, and design: the place where most organisations struggle and the best products are born.

At Apple I trained Geniuses and built multilingual learning content and platforms from scratch. At Anaplan I ran global content services (research, taxonomy, technical content, digital experiences, community, and localization) and engineered the systems underneath. At Snyk I led R&D content services: technical docs, product learning, and support deflection, with AI-assisted workflows for PM communications. At Qatalog I partnered with the CEO on marketing and comms and grew output 400%. Now at Intuit I lead content designers across four continents, running content operations and accelerating them with AI agents and intake automation.

Through all of it I've stayed hands-on. I'm comfortable in the terminal, I build web applications, and I can prototype the thing or describe it. That's what lets me lead engineers, designers, writers, PMs, and localization teams credibly: I've done each of their jobs.
