Intuit · 2026 · Applied AI

The 3-month project that took 4 hours

QuickBooks ships localized content experiences to millions of small businesses worldwide. The traditional pipeline (research, content, design, and front-end build) ran for roughly three months per initiative.

Intuit · 2026 · Content operations

One front door for every content request

Requests arrived from every direction: incomplete briefs, endless email threads, and tickets landing on the wrong team. Content designers spent more time chasing context than creating.

  • What I did: built a web intake form that asks the right questions based on content type (article, video, AI ad, and more), so requestors arrive with a complete brief.
  • How: conversational, step-by-step flow with conditional fields; on submit, it generates a Jira ticket and routes it to the correct global team by content type and region.
  • Result: less back-and-forth between requestors and designers, faster triage, and more time freed for creators to actually create.
